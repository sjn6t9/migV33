Privacy Policy

Last updated: 02 November 2026

This policy explains what information MigV33 (the Android app and the website migv33.com) collects, why we use it, who it is shared with, and how you can delete it. Questions: support@migv33.com

1. Information we collect

  • Account: your MigV33 ID (username) and password. The password is handled by Google Firebase Authentication — we never see it. Your recovery email, if you add one. Your Security PIN and security-question answer are stored only as secure hashes.
  • Profile (what you choose to add): profile and cover photo, full name, gender, date of birth, country, relationship status, bio and status message.
  • Your content: messages in chat rooms and private chats, photos and voice messages you send, mail, gifts, reactions and votes.
  • Coins and activity: coin balance and coin history (transfers, gifts, bets and winnings in the in-app games), XP, level, badges and achievements. Coins are virtual and are used only inside MigV33.
  • Device and technical data: your IP address and the approximate location worked out from it (country, region, city, internet provider); on Android, an identifier of your phone that we store only in hashed form; your push-notification token; when you were last online.

2. How we use it

  • To run MigV33: your account, chat rooms, friends, private chat, gifts, coins, games, levels and leaderboards.
  • To keep MigV33 safe: detecting several accounts on one phone or IP address, stopping cheating, spam and abuse, and banning accounts that break the rules.
  • To send you notifications (messages, friend requests, gifts) and the codes you ask for (email verification, password / PIN recovery).
  • To answer you when you contact support.

We do not sell your personal data and we do not use it for advertising.

3. Who it is shared with

We use these services to run MigV33. They receive only what they need:

  • Google Firebase / Google Cloud — sign-in, the database, file storage (photos, voice messages), server functions and push notifications. All the data above is stored and processed there.
  • ipwho.is — your IP address is sent to it to look up the approximate location and internet provider (used for security, see above).
  • Google Gmail — sends the verification and recovery codes to your email address.
  • Vercel — hosts the website; like any website host it receives normal request data such as your IP address.
  • Google reCAPTCHA — may check, on the website, that requests come from a real browser.
  • YouTube — only if you play a YouTube link in a chat, the video is loaded from YouTube.

We may also share information if the law requires it.

4. What other users can see

Your MigV33 ID, profile (unless you lock it), level, badges, online status, and the messages you post in chat rooms are visible to other users. Private chats and mail are visible only to you and the other person.

5. Security

All data travels over encrypted connections (HTTPS / TLS). Access to the database is limited by security rules, and coins move only through our server. PINs and security answers are stored as hashes.

6. How long we keep it, and deleting your account

We keep your data while your account exists. You can delete your account at any time in the app or on the website (Settings → Delete account (under Log out)), or ask us by email (support@migv33.com, subject “Delete my account”, with your MIG ID). It is deleted 7 days later — logging in again within those 7 days cancels it. Your profile, photos, friends, your mailbox, alerts, tags, coins, coin and XP history, IP records and login are deleted.

What we keep after deletion: chat room and private messages you sent (with their photos, voice messages and files) and mail you sent stay for the other people, shown as “Deleted user”; records that belong to other people (for example a gift they received from you) stay without your name; reports and ban records (IP address or device bans) are kept as long as needed to keep MigV33 safe; and a short note that the account was deleted. How to delete your account.

7. Your choices

  • Edit or remove your profile details and photos at any time.
  • Lock your profile, turn off email from other users, and block people (Settings → Privacy).
  • Turn off notifications in your phone or browser settings.
  • Delete your account: Settings → Delete account (under Log out) (see above).

8. Children

MigV33 is only for people 18 and older. If you believe someone under 18 has an account, report them in the app or contact us and we will delete it.

9. Changes

If this policy changes, we will update this page and the date at the top.
