Last updated: 02 November 2026

These terms apply to the MigV33 Android app and the website migv33.com. By creating a MigV33 ID or using MigV33 you agree to them and to our Privacy Policy. Questions: support@migv33.com

1. Who can use MigV33

  • You must be 18 years or older.
  • One person, one account. Making many accounts to cheat, spam or get around a ban is not allowed.
  • You are responsible for your account. Never share your password or Security PIN — the MigV33 team will never ask for them.

2. Community rules — zero tolerance

These are not allowed anywhere in MigV33 — in chat rooms, private chats, mail, photos, voice messages, profiles, status messages or room names:

  • Harassment, bullying, threats, or encouraging anyone to hurt themselves or others.
  • Hate speech or attacks on people because of religion, race, ethnicity, nationality, caste, gender, sexual orientation or disability.
  • Sexual or nude content, and any sexual content involving minors (reported to the authorities).
  • Violent or shocking content, terrorism or extremist content.
  • Scams, fraud, phishing, asking for passwords, PINs or money.
  • Buying, selling or trading MigV33 coins or accounts for real money or anything outside MigV33.
  • Spam, flooding, advertising or links to harmful sites.
  • Pretending to be someone else, including the MigV33 team.
  • Sharing other people's private information or photos without their permission.
  • Anything illegal, including illegal drugs and weapons.

3. Reporting and blocking

  • Report: long-press a room message or a private message and tap 🚩 Report, or open a profile and tap 🚩 Report. Reports are private — the person does not see who reported them.
  • Block: open a profile or a private chat and tap 🚫 Block. You will no longer see their room messages, their private messages or their notifications. You can unblock them any time in Settings → Blocked users.
  • The MigV33 team reviews reports, usually within 24 hours, and can remove content, mute, kick or ban from rooms, and suspend or delete accounts. Serious cases may be reported to the authorities.
  • You can also write to support@migv33.com or use Settings → Help & Support.

4. Room owners and moderators

Room owners and moderators help keep their rooms safe (mute, kick, ban). They must follow these rules too and must not misuse their tools. The MigV33 team can remove a moderator or a room that breaks the rules.

5. Virtual coins, gifts and games

  • MigV33 coins are virtual items for fun inside MigV33 only. They have no real-money value.
  • Coins cannot be bought with real money in MigV33 and can never be exchanged for money, prizes or anything outside MigV33.
  • The games (Dice, TeenPatti, Royale Battle, LowCard, Cricket and others) are played only with these virtual coins. There is no real-money gambling and nothing real can be won.
  • Selling or buying coins, gifts or accounts outside MigV33 is forbidden; accounts that do it can lose their coins or be banned.
  • Coins, gifts and XP can be changed, reset or removed, for example after cheating or a bug.

6. Your content

You own what you post, and you let MigV33 store and show it to the people you send it to so the service works. Do not post anything you do not have the right to share. We may remove any content that breaks these rules.

7. Ending your account

You can ask to delete your account any time — see Delete your account. We may suspend or delete accounts that break these terms.
